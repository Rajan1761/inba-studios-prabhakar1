About The Artist

A Storyteller Behind The Lens

Creative photographer and videographer with extensive experience in photography, cinematography, content production, and channel management.

I'm Prabhakar Inbalagan — a Chennai-based creative director who has spent over a decade framing light, motion and emotion. From leading shoots and managing YouTube content to operating drones for aerial cinematography, my work lives where technical precision meets visual storytelling.

My journey spans weddings, commercial projects, wildlife, and digital media — always chasing that one frame that makes a viewer feel something they can't quite name. This is more than a portfolio. It's a reel of moments I've been honoured to capture.
